01 / About

Questions at the edge of order and complexity.

My work explores how symbolic descriptions, invariant measures, and geometric structure interact in low-dimensional dynamics. I am especially interested in systems with discontinuities or holes, where small changes can create rich bifurcation patterns.

I joined Wuhan University of Technology as a lecturer in 2025 after completing my PhD at South China University of Technology. During 2023–2024, I was a joint PhD student at IRIF, Université Paris Cité, working with Wolfgang Steiner.

02 / Research

What I work on

A connected set of problems in dynamics, geometry, and number theory.

01

Lorenz maps & open systems

Expansive and decreasing Lorenz maps, holes at critical points, renormalization, and bifurcation sets.

02

β-transformations

Intermediate and negative β-shifts, symbolic dynamics, finite-type structure, and invariant measures.

03

Fractals & thermodynamic formalism

Fractal geometry, metric number theory, pressure, entropy, and spectrality of infinite convolutions.
